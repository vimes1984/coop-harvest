### Text Pitch (Post as the first comment on the submission):
```text
Hi HN!

I built Growers' Collective because supermarkets in Ireland capture up to 85% of retail food margins, squeezing local farmers to the brink of extinction. 

This is an open-source, decoupled React/Express monorepo (with Electron for desktop and Capacitor for iOS/Android) that lets local communities run their own Networked CSA hubs using volunteer labour. We route 82% of every Euro directly to the grower, compared to the 15% they receive from supermarket chains.

We have pre-configured this pilot corridor for Ireland (Wicklow, Cork, Galway) partnering with local GAA (Gaelic Athletic Association) clubs as pickup depots.

The codebase is fully dockerized for quick developer quickstarts. Looking for feedback and contributors interested in:
- Localizing payment routing (Stripe connect setup).
- Offline-first database synchronization for remote farm depots.
- Alternative local logistics hub layouts.

GitHub: https://github.com/vimes1984/coop-harvest
Live Demo: https://vimes1984.github.io/coop-harvest/
```

I wanted to share Growers' Collective, a project I've been building to help restructure local agricultural supply chains.
